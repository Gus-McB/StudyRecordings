## 🎮 **Interactive Tutorial: Your First Analysis**

Let's walk through analyzing your first audio recording! 

### 📋 **What You'll Need:**
- Audio files (any format: MP3, WAV, M4A, WMA, AMR)
- 5 minutes of your time
- A cup of coffee ☕ (optional but recommended)

### 🎯 **Step-by-Step Walkthrough:**

#### **Step 1: Launch the App** 🚀
```bash
python gui_app.py
```
*A sleek, modern interface appears with a dark gray theme*

#### **Step 2: Choose Your Method** 🎨

**🖱️ The Drag & Drop Way (Most Fun!):**
1. Open your file explorer
2. Find your audio folder
3. Drag it onto the left panel
4. Watch the interface light up with a ✅

**📁 The Traditional Way:**
1. Click "Browse for Audio Folder"
2. Navigate to your audio files
3. Select the folder
4. Click OK

#### **Step 3: Configure Your Analysis** ⚙️
- ✅ **Transcribe Audio Files** - Convert speech to text
- ✅ **Generate Analysis Reports** - Get ML-powered insights
- 📂 **Output Location** - Default: Desktop/TranscriptAnalysis

#### **Step 4: Hit the Magic Button** ✨
Click **🚀 Start Processing** and watch the show!
